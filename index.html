About Me

Ever since she was a little girl, she’s been drawing girls, giving them a voice through her sketches. As she grew up, she began to dream of seeing a bigger world. Years later, she stepped out into it and made Toronto home. Today she blends everything she brought with her and everything she’s found here to create digital experiences that bring people closer together.

She works across web design and visual communication, with a background in 3D animation. She’s fascinated by composition, space, and the way visual elements relate to one another. Whether she’s arranging a website layout or building a 3D scene, she thinks about how each piece shapes the whole experience, from the first impression to the smallest detail.

Beyond Design

Away from the screen, you’ll find her taking photos with old cameras, styling her living space, keeping a diary, or adding to her collection of quirky little things like Fugglers, those oddly charming plush toys with human teeth. She loves browsing independent gift shops, hunting for new places to eat, and hopes to adopt a cat and a dog someday. Above all, she’s drawn to things with character, thoughtful details, and a touch of nostalgia.
